Hi, I'm Siggie, an artist from South Korea.

Since 2026 I have been studying at the [HfK Bremen](https://www.hfk-bremen.de) (Hochschule für Künste Bremen) in Germany. My work moves between painting, sculpture and performance, and I'm curious about how these different ways of making can feed into one another.

This site is my archive of works and ideas. For questions or collaborations, see the [contact page]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}).
